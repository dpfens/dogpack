Tips
XDoG uses isotropic Gaussian blur - fast and great for most images. FDoG uses flow-guided blur along edge tangents for smoother, more coherent lines. Best for portraits and subjects where line continuity matters. ADoG makes contrast sensitivity tone-adaptive, producing a screentone/halftone effect that gets denser in darker regions. HDoG combines an FDoG line pass with two ADoG screentone passes at different scales, giving coherent lines plus shaded screentone in one image.
